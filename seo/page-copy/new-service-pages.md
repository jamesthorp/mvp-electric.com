## `/panel-upgrades/`

**H1:** Electrical Panel Upgrades in the Portland Metro

Adding an EV charger, heat pump, hot tub or induction range? Breakers that keep tripping, or an
old Federal Pacific or Zinsco panel? MVP Electric upgrades panels and electrical services for homes
and businesses across Washington, Clackamas and Multnomah counties.

### What we do
- 100A to 200A panel and service upgrades
- Panel replacements for outdated or recalled brands
- Subpanels for garages, shops and ADUs
- Load calculations, so you only upgrade if you actually need to
- Coordination with PGE or Pacific Power for disconnect and reconnect
- Permit and inspection included

### Signs you may need a panel upgrade
- Breakers trip often, or the panel feels warm
- You're adding an EV charger, heat pump or other big appliance
- The panel is a Federal Pacific (Stab-Lok), Zinsco or fuse box
- You're out of breaker space and using tandem breakers everywhere

### FAQ
**How long does a panel upgrade take?**
Most residential panel swaps take one day of work. Scheduling the utility disconnect and the
inspection can add a few days.

**How much does a panel upgrade cost in Portland?**
It depends on amperage, meter location and utility requirements. Text photos of your panel and
meter to (503) 922-6873 for a fast ballpark, then we'll confirm with a free on-site estimate.

## `/generator-installation/`

**H1:** Generator Installation & Interlock Kits

Don't sit in the dark during the next ice storm. MVP Electric installs generator interlock kits,
outdoor generator inlets and whole-home standby generators throughout the Portland metro area.

### Options
- **Interlock kit + inlet box:** the safe, code-approved way to power your home's circuits from a
  portable generator. It's the most affordable option.
- **Manual transfer switch:** powers a chosen set of essential circuits.
- **Whole-home standby generator:** turns on automatically when the power goes out.

### Why do it right
Back-feeding a house through a "suicide cord" can kill a utility line worker and destroy your
generator. A permitted interlock prevents that and passes inspection.

### FAQ
**What size generator do I need?**
That depends on what you want to run (furnace, well pump, fridge, etc.). We'll go through it with you during the estimate.

**Is the permit included?**
Yes. Every generator install is permitted and inspected.
