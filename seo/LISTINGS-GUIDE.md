## 1. Bing Places (about 5–10 minutes)

The easy way is to **import from your Google Business Profile**. It copies everything over.

1. Go to **https://www.bingplaces.com** and click **Sign in** (or **Get Started**).
2. Sign in with **Google** (the same Google account that manages your Business Profile).
3. Choose **Import from Google Business Profile**, select **MVP Electric**, and confirm.
4. Check the imported info against the table above. Fix anything that's different (hours, website,
   description).
5. Submit. Listings imported from a verified Google profile are usually verified automatically.
   If Bing asks for verification, pick phone or email.

If the import option doesn't appear: choose **Add new business**, search "MVP Electric Portland",
claim it if found, otherwise create it with the details above.

## 2. Bing Webmaster Tools (optional, 3 minutes, helps ChatGPT/Copilot find your pages)

1. Go to **https://www.bing.com/webmasters** and sign in with the same Google account.
2. Choose **Import from Google Search Console** if you use Search Console. Otherwise add
   `https://mvp-electric.com` and pick **HTML meta tag** verification. **Send me the tag and I'll add
   it to your site.**
3. Under **Sitemaps**, submit: `https://mvp-electric.com/sitemap_index.xml`

## 3. Apple Business Connect (about 10 minutes + verification)

1. Go to **https://businessconnect.apple.com** and sign in with your **Apple ID** (the one on your iPhone).
2. Click **Add a Location** (or **Get Started**), then search for "MVP Electric".
   - If it's found: click **Claim**.
   - If not: choose **Add new location** and enter the details from the table above.
3. Verify ownership. Apple offers a phone call to your business number or uploading a business
   document (e.g. your CCB license or a utility bill). The phone call is fastest.
4. After it's verified, fill in:
   - **Hours**, **website**, **category**, and the **description** above (Apple calls it "About").
   - **Photos:** a cover photo (the battery install looks great) plus the others.
   - **Action link:** choose "Get a quote" or "Website" → `https://mvp-electric.com/contact-us/`
5. Apple reviews changes, so it can take a few days before it shows in Apple Maps and Siri.
